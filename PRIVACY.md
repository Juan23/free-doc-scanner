# Free Doc Scanner privacy policy

Last updated: 3 November 2026

Free Doc Scanner does not collect, store on any server, share or sell any personal data. It has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking, and it makes no network requests.

## What happens on your phone

- **Scanning** uses the app's own camera screen. Scanning happens on the device. On Android, Google Play services may download Google's scanning components that come with the app's camera library; that download is between your phone and Google and is covered by Google's privacy policy.
- **Your scans** are kept inside the app on your phone until you delete them. On Android they are excluded from Google's device backup. On iPhone, your own iCloud Backup, if you have it turned on, includes the app's data like any other app's.
- **Saving** puts PDFs in a folder you choose and JPEGs in Photos or your gallery. After that, those files are yours and the app does not track them.

## Permissions

- **Camera:** used only while you scan.
- **Add to Photos:** used only when you tap Save JPEG. The app never reads your photo library.

## Children

The app collects no data from anyone, including children.
